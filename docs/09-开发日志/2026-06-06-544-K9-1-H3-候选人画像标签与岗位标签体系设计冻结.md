# K9-1-H3：候选人画像标签与岗位标签体系设计冻结

## 背景

K9-1-H2 诊断：5层联动失效，缺少标签体系。

## 设计冻结

- **CandidateTag**: 5组（Direction/Capability/Domain/Evidence/Risk）
- **Confidence**: 0-3，短token≤1，长语义≤3
- **Dominant Profile**: algorithm_research/backend_engineering/career_exploration 等
- **JobTag**: 4组（Direction/Domain/Capability/Requirement）
- **标签权重矩阵**: recommendation_system×18, research_ability×technical_research×14, engineering_ability×backend_service×10 等
- **冲突规则**: algorithm_research主画像下 backend_service 不得≥high
- **Label升级**: f(score, direction_fit, tag_fit, risk_level)
- **Judge升级**: 检查 dominant_profile×top1, label←→risk
