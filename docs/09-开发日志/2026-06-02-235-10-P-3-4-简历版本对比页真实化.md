## 3. 关键发现

**前后端类型契约不匹配：** 前端 `ResumeVersionComparison` 类型定义 `{ base_resume, target_resume, summary, keyword_changes, project_changes, remaining_risks, recommendation }` 与后端实际返回 `{ compare_id, generated_by, result: { score_before/after/change, field_changes, risk_changes, suggestion_completion, key_improvements, remaining_risks, summary, authenticity_notice }, created_at }` 完全不同。需要修复类型对齐。
